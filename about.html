About LoHi Cleaning

Mike Aube founded LoHi Cleaning LLC.

With the determination to deliver the utmost in cleanliness and customer satisfaction, Mike Aube founded LoHi Cleaning LLC.

The name, LoHi in reference to “Lower Highlands,” paid homage to the historic and culturally rich Lower Highlands neighborhood where Mike had lived and witnessed the thriving community spirit. He envisioned his company as a symbol of community unity and growth, where every sparkling clean home and office space contributed to a healthier, happier Denver.

Locally owned and owner-operated since 2021, I (Mike the owner/cleaner) set out to provide the best possible cleaning experience to the wonderful people of Denver, Colorado. Caring for your home is my specialty.

“I promise to provide you the best possible cleaning services in Denver. I understand you have many options for house cleaning in the amazing city of Denver. I assure you will be happy with my attention to detail and guaranteed quality.” — Mike Aube
